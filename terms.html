Terms & Conditions

Last updated: 22 November 2026

By signing in to RRR Tournament ("we", "the site") you accept these terms, our Refund & Withdrawal Policy and our Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the site.

1. Who can use the site

  • You must be 18 years or older. We may close the account of anyone younger.
  • One person can have one account. One Free Fire UID can be linked to only one account. Using fake, borrowed or another person's UID is not allowed.
  • You must give true information (name, UID, IGN, e-Sewa ID) and a real screenshot of your own Free Fire profile. A fake, edited or someone else's screenshot leads to a ban. PC players are not allowed in matches.

2. Coins and your wallet

  • Coins are the in-site balance used for entry fees and prizes. 1 NPR added = 1 coin.
  • Signup bonus coins (25 coins for new players) can be used to join tournaments. They cannot be withdrawn and cannot be used in Game rooms. Coins you add and prizes you win can be withdrawn.
  • Coins have no value outside this site and cannot be transferred to another player.

3. Adding coins

  • Pay by e-Sewa QR (minimum 25 NPR) and submit the correct Reference ID and amount. We check every payment by hand; coins are added within 6-8 hours.
  • A Reference ID can be used only once. Sending a wrong, fake or edited payment is fraud: the request is rejected and the account can be banned.

4. Tournament matches

  • Entry fee is taken from your wallet when you join. Match rules, prize table and times are shown on the site and may be changed by the admin before a match starts.
  • Room ID and password are for the joined player only. Do not share them.
  • Hacks, teaming with other squads, stream sniping, emulator/PC use and abusive behaviour lead to disqualification and ban. The admin's decision is final.

5. Mini games and game rooms

  • Mini games do not need a Free Fire name or UID. Your Google first name and last initial are shown in the room.
  • In a game room every player pays the same entry fee. The prize pool is the total fee minus a platform fee (currently 20%). Prizes are given by the rank shown in the room.
  • Scores are checked by our server. A score that cannot be verified is not counted. Bots, scripts, edited code or sharing answers is cheating.
  • To keep the site fair, a prize may be held for admin review (usually up to 24 hours) when it is large, when your score is unusually high or when you have won a lot in one day. If the review shows fair play, the full prize is added to your wallet.

6. Prizes and withdrawals

  • Minimum withdrawal is 100 coins. Your first withdrawal needs a verified Game Profile. When you create your Game Profile you upload a screenshot of your Free Fire profile (it must show your ID/UID and name). The site reads the UID and name from it; if it cannot be checked automatically, the admin checks it. If you change your UID later, you must upload a new screenshot.
  • Withdrawals are sent to the e-Sewa ID you enter, within 6-8 hours. You are responsible for entering the correct e-Sewa ID.

7. Refund & Withdrawal Policy

  • Cancelled match or room: if the admin cancels a match, or a game room is cancelled or does not fill in time, the entry fee is returned to your wallet.
  • Rejected withdrawal: the coins are returned to your wallet.
  • No refund for: not joining or being late to the room, wrong UID/IGN entered by you, leaving a match, a poor internet connection or device problem, a lost game round, or a ban for cheating or fraud.
  • Added coins: payments are not refunded once the coins are in your wallet. If you paid by mistake or paid twice, contact support within 7 days with the Reference ID and we will check it.
  • Banned for cheating, fake payments or multiple accounts: the wallet balance and held prizes can be cancelled.
  • To raise a problem, contact us on our Discord server (link on the site): open the support channel or create a ticket and send your login email and the Reference ID or match name.

8. Fair play and bans

We may warn, disqualify, hold prizes, ban an account or stop withdrawals if we find cheating, fake payments, duplicate accounts, abusive in-game names or any misuse of the site. We may refuse service to anyone.

A ban can be for the whole site, one mode, one match or the mini games. It can be placed on the account e-mail, on a Free Fire UID, or on an e-Sewa ID (withdrawals to a banned e-Sewa ID are blocked, even from a new account). Mini game bans use the account e-mail only. Trying to get around a ban with a new account is a reason for a permanent ban.

9. Closing your account

You can ask us to close your account at any time through support. Withdraw your coins first. See the Privacy Policy for how your data is handled.
