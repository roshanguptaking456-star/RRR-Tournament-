Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

RRR Tournament ("we", "our", "the site") is a website where players join Free Fire tournaments and play mini games (Rail Rush, Memory Match, Target Tap) using in-site coins. This page explains what information we collect, why we collect it and what choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Google sign-in: when you tap "Continue with Google" we receive your name, email address and profile picture from Google. We never see your Google password.
  • Site password (optional): if you set a password for logging in without Google, we store only a scrambled (hashed) form of it, never the password itself.
  • Game details you enter: your In-Game Name (IGN), Free Fire UID, and teammate names/UIDs for squad matches. The optional Game Profile (IGN and UID) in your Account page is used only in the Games section.
  • Wallet and payment details: your coin balance, payment reference IDs you submit when you add coins, and the e-Sewa ID you give us to send withdrawals.
  • Activity on the site: matches joined, game rooms created or joined, scores, rewards, notifications, history, and referral information.

2. How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account and wallet safe.
  • To register you in matches and game rooms, calculate results and give rewards.
  • To check payments and send withdrawals to your e-Sewa.
  • To show your history and notifications, and to run referrals.
  • To find cheating, fake scores, duplicate accounts and misuse, and to ban such accounts.

3. Where it is stored

Your information is stored in Google Sheets and processed by Google Apps Script that are controlled by the site owner. Sign-in is handled by Google. The website itself is hosted on Netlify. These providers may process data under their own privacy policies.

4. Sharing

We do not sell your information. Other players can see only what is needed to run a match or room, such as your IGN, your score and your rank. We may share information if the law requires it or to protect the site from fraud.

5. Cookies and local storage

The site keeps a sign-in token and a few settings (for example your last used IGN) in your browser storage so you stay signed in. We do not use advertising cookies.

6. How long we keep it

We keep account, wallet and match records for as long as your account is active and as needed to settle payments and disputes.

7. Your choices

  • You can change or remove your IGN and UID in the Account page.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and data. Contact us using the social links shown on the site (WhatsApp, Messenger, Telegram, etc.) and send the Google email you signed in with. Coins that are still in your wallet may need to be withdrawn first.
  • You can remove the site's access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account security settings.

8. Age

RRR Tournament is meant for people aged 18 or older. If we learn that a younger person is using paid features, we may close the account.

9. Not affiliated

RRR Tournament is an independent community site. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Garena or Free Fire. All game names and logos belong to their owners.

10. Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.

11. Contact

For any privacy question or deletion request, contact us through the social links shown on the RRR Tournament site.
