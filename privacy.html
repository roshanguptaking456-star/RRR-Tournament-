Privacy Policy

RRR Tournament ("we", "our", "the site") runs online Free Fire tournaments. Last updated: 30 October 2026.

What information we collect

  • Your Google account name and email address, received when you sign in with Google.
  • Your Free Fire UID, in-game name and teammate details, when you join a match.
  • e-Sewa reference IDs, amounts and e-Sewa IDs, when you add money or request a withdrawal.

We do not receive your Google password. We only use the basic profile and email permissions.

How we use it

  • To log you in and keep your coin balance.
  • To register you for matches, run them, and give prizes.
  • To verify payments and process withdrawals.

Storage and sharing

Your data is stored in a private Google Sheet that only the site admin can open. We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers. We may share it only if required by law.

Deleting your data

To see or delete your data, email us at the address below. Your coin balance and entry records will be removed on request.
